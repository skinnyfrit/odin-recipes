Lasagna

(Photo by joe boshra on Unsplash)

Description

A deliciously layered Italian dish and a favourite of one of my beloved childhood character's.
How hard could layering pasta, meat and cheese be? Well, let's find out in this recipe!

Ingredients

  • Lasagna pasta sheet
  • Can of pureed tomatoes
  • Basil
  • Minced beef
  • Parmigiano reggiano (Parmesan)
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Garlic
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Gather your ingredients.
  2. Stir fry the garlic until golden brown.
  3. Add the minced beef until browned.
  4. Stir in the pureed tomatoes.
  5. Bring a pot of lightly salted water to boil.
  6. Cook lasagna sheets till al dente, following packaging instructions (if any).
  7. Preheat oven.
  8. To assemble, spread a layer of meat sauce over the bottom of a baking dish. Then, layer a sheet of lasagne over the sauce, ensuring they overlap slightly when covering the sauce. Finally, cover the lasagna sheet with mozzarella and grated parmesan.
  9. Repeat the layering step until sufficiently filled, or once ingredients are used up. Top the final layer with more cheese.
  10. Cover the baking dish with aluminum foil and send that bad boy to the oven for 25 minutes.
  11. Remove the foil, then bake for another 25 minutes.
  12. Let the lasagna rest before cutting it.
