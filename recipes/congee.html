Congee

(Photo by Erik Mclean on Unsplash)

Description

A simple yet comforting dish that reminds me of love and a home, especially whenever I fall ill.
Simplicity might seem boring, but it opens the doors to creativity. Add on other side dishes to pair with this dish, and you might find a door to customising your favourite congee!

Ingredients

  • Rice - 1 cup
  • Water - 2 level

Steps

  1. Pour rice and water into a pot.
  2. Boil it, letting the lid cover the pot partially. :warning: Do not leave a boiling pot unattended!
  3. Once the pot comes to a rolling boil and bubbles, remove the lid. Continue to boil for 5 minutes, stirring occassionally.
  4. Pair with any side dishes you prefer!
