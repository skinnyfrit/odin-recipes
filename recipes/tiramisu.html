Tiramisu

(Photo by Jay on Unsplash)

Description

A deliciously layered Italian dessert, with a positively positive meaning for the weary. This dish is one of my favourites!
Some say that tiramisu is just like lasagna; except it's a dessert! Could the difficulty be just as hard as the lasagna? Let's find out!

Ingredients

  • Ladyfingers biscuits
  • Mascarpone cheese
  • Cream cheese
  • Cocoa powder
  • Hot espresso

Steps

  1. Gather your ingredients.
  2. Mix the mascarpone cheese and cream cheese together.
  3. Soak the biscuits in espresso for 2 seconds on each side. Then, place them in a dish, filling the bottom.
  4. Cover the soaked biscuits with the cheese mixture, making sure there is an appropriate width of filling.
  5. Dust cocoa powder to finish one layer.
  6. Repeat this process until the final layer, ensuring the cocoa powder layers the top in a smooth and even fashion.
  7. Chill in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
  8. Remove from the fridge and dig in!
